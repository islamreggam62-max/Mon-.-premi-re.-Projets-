Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Shipping and billing address
  • Order contents and order history
  • Messages you send to customer support

We do not store your full card number. Card details are collected and processed directly by our payment provider and never reach our servers.

Information collected automatically

  • Device type, browser, operating system and IP address

  • Cookies and similar technologies

How we use your information

  • To process, fulfil and deliver your order
  • To send order confirmations, dispatch notices and tracking updates
  • To respond to your support requests
  • To detect and prevent fraud
  • To improve our store and product range
  • To send marketing messages — only if you opted in, and you can unsubscribe at any time

Who we share it with

We share only what is necessary, with:

  • Shopify — our e-commerce platform, which hosts the store and processes orders
  • Payment providers — to process your payment securely
  • Suppliers and fulfilment partners — your name, address and phone number, so your order can be shipped
  • Shipping carriers — to deliver your parcel and provide tracking
  • Analytics and advertising providers — where you have consented to those cookies

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies

We use cookies that are strictly necessary for the store to work (cart, checkout, security), and — with your consent — analytics and advertising cookies. You can refuse or delete cookies in your browser settings, but the store may not function correctly without the necessary ones.

Data retention

We keep order records for as long as required to fulfil orders, handle returns and meet legal and accounting obligations. Marketing contact details are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You may request to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, and you may object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, email [[SUPPORT_EMAIL]]. We will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Security

The store runs over encrypted HTTPS connections and payments are handled by PCI-DSS compliant providers. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children

Our store is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
