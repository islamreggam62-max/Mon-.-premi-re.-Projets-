Refund & Return Policy

We want you to be satisfied with your purchase. If something is not right, this page explains exactly what to do.

Return window

You may request a return within [[RETURN_WINDOW]] days of receiving your order.

Conditions for return

To be eligible, the item must be:

  • Unused and in the same condition in which you received it
  • In its original packaging with all accessories and manuals included
  • Accompanied by proof of purchase (order number or receipt)

Items that cannot be returned

  • Personal care and hair-care devices that have been used, for hygiene reasons — unless the item is faulty
  • Items damaged through misuse, accident or normal wear
  • Items returned without prior authorisation
  • Gift cards and clearance items marked final sale

How to start a return

  1. Email [[SUPPORT_EMAIL]] with your order number and the reason for the return. Include photos if the item is damaged or faulty.
  2. We will reply within 2 business days with return authorisation and the return address.
  3. Ship the item back using a tracked service and send us the tracking number.

Do not send an item back before receiving return authorisation — unauthorised returns cannot be processed.

Return shipping costs

  • Faulty, damaged or incorrect item: we cover return shipping in full.
  • Change of mind: return shipping is the customer's responsibility.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, we will notify you of the outcome. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. The time it takes to appear on your statement depends on your bank or card issuer.

Original shipping charges, where paid, are non-refundable except when the return is due to our error.

Damaged or faulty on arrival

Contact us within 48 hours of delivery at [[SUPPORT_EMAIL]] with photos of the item and the packaging. We will arrange a replacement or a full refund at no cost to you.

Wrong item received

If you received an item you did not order, contact us within 7 days. We will send the correct item and arrange collection of the incorrect one at our expense.

Order cancellation

You may cancel an order free of charge at any time before it is dispatched. Once dispatched, the order falls under the return process above.

Exchanges

We do not process direct exchanges. Return the original item for a refund and place a new order for the item you want.
