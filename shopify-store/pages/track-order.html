Don't have a tracking number yet?

Tracking numbers are sent by email and SMS as soon as your order is dispatched — normally within [[PROCESSING_DAYS]] business days of ordering. If it's been longer than that, contact us.

Tracking shows no information

This is normal for the first 24 to 72 hours after dispatch. The carrier needs to scan the parcel into its network before updates appear. If there's still nothing after 5 business days, email us and we'll chase it.

Tracking hasn't updated in several days

Parcels often sit without a scan while in transit between countries or in customs clearance. This is normal and usually resolves itself. If tracking hasn't moved in 10 business days, contact us and we'll open an investigation with the carrier.
