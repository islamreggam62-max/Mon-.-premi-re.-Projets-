Frequently Asked Questions

Orders

How do I know my order went through?

You'll get a confirmation email within a few minutes of ordering. If it hasn't arrived, check your spam folder, then contact us at [[SUPPORT_EMAIL]] with the email address you used.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes, free of charge, any time before it's dispatched. Email us as quickly as possible with your order number. Once a parcel has left the warehouse we can't recall it, but you can return it under our Refund Policy.

Can I change my shipping address after ordering?

Only before dispatch. Contact us immediately with your order number and the corrected address.

Shipping

Where do you ship?

Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates.

How long does delivery take?

[[PROCESSING_DAYS]] business days to process, then [[DELIVERY_WINDOW]] business days for delivery. Full details on our Shipping Policy page.

How much is shipping?

Standard shipping is free on all orders to Saudi Arabia and the UAE.

How do I track my order?

Use your tracking number on our Track Order page. Tracking can take 24 to 72 hours to become active after dispatch.

Will I pay customs or import duties?

Any duties or taxes charged by the destination country are the recipient's responsibility. See our Shipping Policy.

My order arrived in two parcels — is that normal?

Yes. Multi-item orders sometimes ship separately at no extra cost. You'll receive a tracking number for each parcel.

Payment

Which payment methods do you accept?

The payment methods shown at checkout. Prices are displayed in your local currency where available.

Is checkout secure?

Yes. The store runs on encrypted HTTPS and payments are processed by a PCI-DSS compliant provider. We never see or store your full card number.

In which currency am I charged?

The currency shown at checkout. If your card is issued in a different currency, your bank may apply its own conversion rate.

Returns and refunds

What's your return policy?

[[RETURN_WINDOW]] days from delivery, for unused items in their original packaging. Full details on our Refund Policy page.

My item arrived damaged. What now?

Email [[SUPPORT_EMAIL]] within 48 hours with photos of the item and the packaging. We'll send a replacement or refund you in full at no cost.

How long does a refund take?

5 to 10 business days to the original payment method once we've received and inspected the return. Your bank may take a few days more to show it.

Products

Do your products come with a warranty?

All products are covered against manufacturing defects within the return window. Warranty terms beyond that are listed on each product page where applicable.

Are the product photos accurate?

Yes, but colours can look different on different screens and packaging can vary between batches.
