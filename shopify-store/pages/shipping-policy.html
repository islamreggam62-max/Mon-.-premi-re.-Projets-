Delivery times are estimates from the date of dispatch, not the date of order. They do not include processing time and may be affected by customs clearance, carrier delays, weather or public holidays.

Tracking

You will receive a tracking number by email and SMS as soon as your order is dispatched. Tracking information can take 24 to 72 hours to become active with the carrier. You can follow your order at any time on our Track Order page.

Split shipments

If you order multiple items, they may arrive in separate parcels on different dates at no extra cost to you. You will receive a tracking number for each parcel.

Customs, duties and taxes

Any import duties, customs fees or taxes applied by the destination country are the responsibility of the recipient. [[STORE_NAME]] has no control over these charges and cannot predict their amount.

Incorrect addresses

Please check your shipping address and phone number carefully before completing your order. We are not able to redirect a parcel once it has been dispatched. If a parcel is returned to us because of an incorrect or incomplete address, we will contact you to arrange reshipment; reshipping costs may apply.

Failed or refused delivery

Carriers normally make several delivery attempts and will contact you on the phone number provided. If a parcel is refused or goes unclaimed and is returned to origin, a refund may be issued less any shipping costs already incurred.

Lost or delayed parcels

If your order has not arrived within 10 business days of the end of the estimated delivery window, contact us at [[SUPPORT_EMAIL]] with your order number. We will open an investigation with the carrier and either reship your order or issue a full refund.
