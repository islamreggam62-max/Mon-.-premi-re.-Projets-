## FAQ (product page accordion)

**Q: Will it make my hair shorter?**
A: No. It removes only the damaged tips at the setting you choose — 3, 6 or 9 mm. That is far less than the 3–5 cm a salon typically cuts.

**Q: Does it work on curly hair?**
A: Yes, but straighten or fully blow-dry the section first. The trimmer needs the hair to feed through the guide in a straight line to find the split ends.

**Q: Is it safe for coloured or keratin-treated hair?**
A: Yes. It cuts the hair shaft mechanically and uses no heat and no chemicals, so it does not affect colour or treatments.

**Q: How often should I use it?**
A: Every 6 to 8 weeks for most people. Heat-styled or bleached hair may need it every 4 to 6 weeks.

**Q: Can I use it on wet hair?**
A: No. Hair must be completely dry and brushed out, or it will not feed correctly and the cut will be uneven.

**Q: How long does the blade last?**
A: Typically 6 to 12 months with regular home use. A spare blade is included in the box.
