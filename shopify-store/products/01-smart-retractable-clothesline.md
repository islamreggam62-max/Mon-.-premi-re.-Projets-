## FAQ (product page accordion)

**Q: Will the adhesive hold on a tiled or painted wall?**
A: Yes — the included pads are rated for smooth, clean, dry surfaces such as tile, glass, painted concrete and finished wood. Clean the surface with alcohol first and let it cure for 24 hours before loading. For rough or textured walls, use the included screws.

**Q: Can I leave it outside in the sun?**
A: Yes. The housing and line are UV-resistant and made for balcony use. To maximise lifespan, retract the line when it's not in use.

**Q: How much laundry can it actually hold?**
A: A full standard washing-machine load — approximately 20 kg of wet clothes across the full 4.2 m of line.

**Q: Does the line sag when loaded?**
A: Some sag is normal with any clothesline. Keep the span under 4 m and tension the line firmly at the hook to minimise it.

**Q: Can I install it myself?**
A: Yes. Adhesive mounting takes about 5 minutes with no tools. Screw mounting takes about 10 minutes with a drill.
