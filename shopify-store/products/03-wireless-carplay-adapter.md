## FAQ (product page accordion)

**Q: Will it work in my car?**
A: If your car has factory wired Apple CarPlay — you currently plug your iPhone in with a cable and CarPlay appears on the screen — then yes. If your car does not have CarPlay at all, this adapter cannot add it.

**Q: Does it work with Android phones?**
A: No. This adapter supports Apple CarPlay only. It does not support Android Auto.

**Q: Do I need to pair it every time?**
A: No. You pair once. After that it connects automatically within about 5 to 10 seconds of starting the car.

**Q: Will it drain my phone battery?**
A: It uses slightly more battery than wired CarPlay because it runs over Wi-Fi. On a long drive, use a wireless charger or a separate charging cable.

**Q: Is there a delay in navigation or music?**
A: No noticeable delay on the 5 GHz connection. Audio and maps run in real time.

**Q: Does it work if more than one person uses the car?**
A: Yes. The adapter stores multiple paired iPhones and connects to whichever one is present.
